FAQs

Questions we hear often

Can't find your answer? Call or WhatsApp us and an engineer will help.

How do I know what solar capacity is right for my home or business?

Our team reviews electricity bills, installation space, electrical requirements and future energy needs to recommend an appropriately sized system.

Are government subsidies available for residential solar installations?

Eligible residential consumers may receive assistance under applicable government solar schemes, including PM Surya Ghar. Eligibility and amounts depend on current scheme rules and system specifications.

Does Sinelab provide assistance with solar financing?

Sinelab can guide customers through available financing options and documentation, subject to lender eligibility, terms and approval.

What is the difference between on-grid and hybrid solar?

An on-grid system works alongside the utility grid and generally does not provide backup during a power outage. A hybrid system can incorporate battery storage to support selected loads during outages, depending on its design.

Does Sinelab provide maintenance and after-sales support?

Yes. Sinelab offers operations and maintenance services, including system inspection, performance support and technical assistance according to the agreed service scope.

Does Sinelab undertake large commercial and industrial projects?

Yes. Sinelab undertakes solar EPC projects for residential, commercial and industrial applications, including rooftop and ground-mounted installations.
