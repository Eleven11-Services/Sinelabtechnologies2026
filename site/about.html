Our story

From classmates to industry leaders

Some journeys begin with a business plan. Ours began with two classmates who shared an ambition to build something meaningful.

In 1996, Sajeev Kumar K and Sunil Joseph founded SARK Cables in Kanjikode. In 2013, that experience gave rise to Sinelab Technologies, first lighting major junctions and public spaces across Kerala, and now powering homes, businesses and industries with solar and storage.

Milestones

Three decades of progress

From a cable plant in Kanjikode to megawatt-scale solar. Scroll through the journey.

  1. 1996

    Where it began

    SARK Cables established in Kanjikode by Sajeev Kumar K and Sunil Joseph.

  2. 2002

    Growing to Thodupuzha

    Manufacturing expands to a plant in Thodupuzha.

  3. 2008

    Into Coimbatore

    Manufacturing expands to a facility in Coimbatore.

  4. 2013

    Sinelab is born

    Sinelab Technologies is founded as an energy-focused venture, starting with industrial lighting, high-mast and streetlighting systems and solar streetlight batteries.

  5. 2018

    Recognition

    The founders’ entrepreneurial journey is recognised with the Asianet Keerthimudra Award in the Electrical and Electronics sector.

  6. 2019

    Power for a community

    A government 20 kW off-grid solar project brings dependable power to a tribal village in the Nelliampathy Hills.

  7. 2024

    Storage, made in-house

    A Kanjikode facility for lithium batteries and integrated lithium inverter solutions.

  8. 2025

    Megawatt-scale

    Approximately 4–5 MW of solar installations commissioned in a single year.

  9. 2026

    A flagship 1 MW rooftop

    Installation completed on a flagship 1 MW rooftop project. Commissioning pending.

Our journey began with lighting the way. Today, we are helping power the future. And the journey continues.
